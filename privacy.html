Privacy Policy – Samuel Fiddes Uploader

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Samuel Fiddes Uploader ("the App") is a private tool used by Samuel Fiddes to publish his own videos to his own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data we access

  • Basic account information (such as display name and open ID) via the user.info.basic scope, only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used to upload and publish videos via the video.upload and video.publish scopes.

2. How data is used

Data is used exclusively to upload and publish videos to the connected TikTok account. The App does not read, collect or analyze other users' data, followers, messages or viewing behavior.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are never sent to any third party or external server other than TikTok's official API.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, shared or disclosed to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as needed to publish videos. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions, after which locally stored tokens become invalid and are deleted.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data of anyone under 18.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The latest version is always available on this page.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent via github.com/SamuelIFiddes.
